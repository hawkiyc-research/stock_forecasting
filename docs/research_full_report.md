## 繁體中文

### 1. 摘要

這是一個為期約兩週（2026 年 10 月 16 日至 29 日）、全程以事前登記方式進行的量化研究專案。起點是「微調一個金融時間序列基礎模型來預測台股個股報酬」；終點是一套在真實成本與執行限制下回測、並在一段完全未看過的樣本外期間接受確認的交易策略。中間經過七個階段、二十個登記過的實驗，大多數假說被否決，每一次否決都改變了下一步的方向。

最後的結果：

- **模型**：LightGBM 橫斷面排序模型，用 183 欄價量特徵加 67 欄免費公開的籌碼特徵，預測每檔股票未來 5 到 10 天扣除市場、市值與產業效應後的相對報酬；每季只用過去資料重新訓練。加入籌碼後排序能力顯著提升（5 到 10 日 Rank IC +0.017，95% 區間不跨 0）。
- **策略**：三種——多日多空、多空加台指期避險、週頻低換手多頭。在 2023 年 1 月至 2026 年 5 月的樣本內，機構成本下年化淨報酬 +19% 到 +52%、淨 Sharpe 1.4 到 2.4。
- **事前登記的選定規則**選出「價量＋籌碼模型 × 週頻低換手多頭」（樣本內 Sharpe 2.38、年化 +51.8%）。
- **樣本外確認（2026 年 6 月至 10 月，85 個交易日）未通過**：淨報酬 +3.5% 為正，但最大回撤 −25.7% 比樣本內的 −22.6% 深。原因是 7 月一次 −17% 的市場修正直接打在 100% 多頭的策略上；同一窗口市場中性的多空策略 +23% 到 +34%，但依事前規則不能事後換選。**沒有策略進入模擬交易。**

這份報告記錄完整的過程：每個階段做了什麼、為什麼失敗、學到什麼，以及最後那個「規則選出來的策略為什麼過不了確認」。對讀者最有用的部分可能是第 4 節的研究歷程與第 8 節的心得。

### 2. 問題定義與目標

**問題**：在嚴格的時間點輸入、次日開盤成交與時間順序評估下，日線價量加免費籌碼資料的橫斷面排序模型，能不能在台灣的交易成本與執行限制下，變成一條可進入模擬交易的策略？

**成功標準（事前登記）**：機構主要成本、分數位置下，樣本內淨 Sharpe ≥ 1.0、淨報酬 95% 區間下界 > 0、樣本內後段淨報酬 > 0；樣本外確認：淨報酬 > 0 且最大回撤不比樣本內深。散戶成本一律並列。

**明確不做的事**：不買付費資料（使用者於 10 月 28 日決定）；不用大型語言模型自動挖因子；當沖路線在成本證據出來後排除；「勝過 0050 買進持有」自 10 月 26 日起不再是門檻——2023 年以來的台股是權值股單邊行情，被動持有與量化交易是不同的賽道。

### 4. 研究歷程：七個階段

#### 4.1 起點：微調金融時序基礎模型（10 月 16 日以前）

用 Kronos（在 K 線資料上預訓練的 transformer）加 LoRA 微調，預測美股與台股個股未來 1 到 14 天的超額報酬與漲跌方向。結果：三個隨機種子在測試期都沒有穩健優勢。誤差拆解顯示問題不在模型容量：alpha 預測的誤差幾乎全在同一天的橫斷面上，日期層級只占 3% 到 4%；方向分類器輸在「每天猜整體漲跌」這件學不來的事，把擇時拿掉後與梯度提升樹持平。

第一次回測（固定持有天數、買賣緩衝）全部失敗：驗證期選出的 10 日設定在測試期年化淨超額 −37% 到 −44%；前幾名股票的優勢只在交易時段（開盤到收盤 +18 到 +31 bps），收盤到隔天開盤反過來虧。一段 2026 年 6 到 9 月的前瞻檢驗也沒有一個訊號的區間排除 0。

**學到**：模型有「同一天分辨相對強弱」的能力，沒有「預測明天漲跌」的能力；問題要重新定義成橫斷面排序。

#### 4.2 改成橫斷面排序任務（10 月 20 日至 21 日）

標籤改成同一天、同一市場內的排名。五個基線（PatchTST、GRU、梯度提升樹、DLinear、均線交叉）的排序能力從驗證到測試大多衰退；Kronos 微調後台股 1 日排序能力 0.064（區間 0.027 到 0.102），**但沒有勝過 PatchTST**（配對差 −0.002，區間跨 0）。

**學到**：昂貴的基礎模型不是這個問題的骨幹；排序能力來自特徵與橫斷面建模。從此以 GPU 訓練時間為成本單位做決策。

#### 4.3 台股主線：特徵梯度提升樹對跨股票 Transformer（10 月 22 日至 23 日）

路線決定後，用同一套 walk-forward 配對比較兩個模型：183 欄手寫特徵的 LightGBM，和一個 MASTER 式的跨股票 transformer（同一天的股票彼此 attention）。LightGBM 短天期排序能力 0.118、5 到 10 日 0.086；transformer 0.122、0.092，差距 +0.004 來自三個種子集成，不是股票間 attention（消融實驗證明）。

第一個策略（每天在 50 檔裡換掉 5 檔）在台股成本下失敗：每天 10% 換手的成本約 17%／年，高於毛超額。

**學到**：深度學習在這個資料規模上的增量不值 GPU 成本；策略層的成本比模型多 0.004 的排序能力重要得多。

#### 4.4 策略層第一輪：買賣緩衝、期貨對沖、當沖（10 月 23 日）

把每日換股改成「買進前 50 名、掉出前 150 名才賣、每天最多換 5 檔」的緩衝區規則，換手降到每天 2% 到 2.5%、成本拖累 3% 到 5%／年；樣本內前段淨超額 +5%，**但 2025 年 6 月之後毛超額 −27% 到 −31%**，整段仍然失敗。用台指期對沖的版本 −12.5%／年、28 次追繳（用市值加權指數對沖一個接近等權的組合，在權值股獨漲的行情裡是錯的工具）。

另一條路線：模型的 1 日訊號在股票期貨上做空方當沖。以期交所官方開盤與結算價計算毛報酬 +61.5%／年，但一個 tick 的滑價每邊約 0.23%，淨 −63%／年——價差把它吃光。

**學到**：2025 年後失效的原因還沒找到；當沖路線被執行成本否決，不是被模型否決。

#### 4.5 殘差化標籤（10 月 23 日至 24 日）

診斷發現 2025 年 6 月之後的失效來自大型股集中行情：把報酬對市場、市值與產業做橫斷面回歸後，模型對**殘差**的 5 到 10 日排序能力在後段仍是 0.065（區間 0.046 到 0.086），對原始報酬的排序只剩前段的四到五成。於是標籤改成殘差化報酬重訓：短天期排序能力不變，後段 5 到 10 日提高 +0.009（區間 +0.006 到 +0.013）。之後所有模型都用這個標籤。凍結預訓練編碼器（Kronos-small、Chronos-2）的跨股票模型重訓後都不優於從頭訓練的版本，深度學習路線退出主線。

**學到**：失效是標籤帶了市值與產業的 β，不是模型忘了怎麼選股。

#### 4.6 暴露診斷與正交化（10 月 24 日至 26 日）

這是整個專案最關鍵的一步。把平滑後的 10 日分數拿去和 60 日 beta、前 20 日與前 60 日報酬算相關：−0.24 到 −0.45 對 beta、−0.1 到 −0.26 對動能——模型分數隱含「低 beta、反動能」的傾斜，在 2025 年後的動能行情裡，多空兩腿同時站錯邊（空頭腿正好放空了大型權值股，每年虧 61% 到 89%）。

把分數**每天對這三個暴露做橫斷面回歸取殘差**（沒有任何擬合參數，band 參數不動），同一個多空策略在機構成本下由上市 −6.3% 變成 **+17.3%**（區間 −0.6% 到 +37.3%）、上櫃 +19.4%、兩市場各半 +18.3%，2025 年後全部轉正，組合 β 由 −0.5 降到 −0.2；加台指期避險再多 +2% 到 +9%。接著把放空腿「全部可借券」的假設換成真實的可借券名單、交易所限額、借不到機率與分層借券費：兩市場各半 +15.3%（區間 +2.5% 到 +30.3%），名單本身幾乎不改變結果，分層費與借不到各吃掉 1 到 3 個百分點。

同時測了「0050 核心加多空疊加」：24 個格子全部輸給 0050 買進持有（同期年化 +46%）。使用者決定這個門檻不再適用——被動持有權值股與量化交易是不同賽道。

**學到**：策略層的失敗先查暴露，再怪模型。正交化是看過後段資料之後決定的設計，所以樣本內的數字只能當資訊，必須另找樣本外確認——這就是第 6 節的由來。

#### 4.7 免費籌碼資料（10 月 25 日至 28 日）

建了 67 欄籌碼特徵（五類法人買賣超的多視窗累計比與 z 分數、融資融券與借券餘額變化、當沖比、注意與處置狀態），公布時刻用保守規則。配對消融：加入籌碼後 5 到 10 日的殘差排序能力 +0.017（區間 +0.013 到 +0.021）、前後四分位 10 日價差 0.65% → 1.04%，兩道事前登記的門檻都通過；只用籌碼、不用價量的模型在 2025 年後的 5 到 10 日排序能力（0.090）甚至超過 183 欄價量（0.065）。增量在近期強勢股裡更大，但兩期、兩組都顯著，所以是一個獨立訊號，不只是條件化反轉。

使用者決定：先不購買付費的券商分點資料，用手上的模型做策略與樣本外確認。

### 5. 最終模型與策略設計

**模型**：LightGBM 排序模型，兩個版本配對——價量模型（183 欄）與價量＋籌碼模型（250 欄）；殘差化標籤；三個種子集成；16 折季度 walk-forward（2016 起擴張訓練）。排序能力（樣本內 791 日）：價量模型短天期 0.117、5 到 10 日 0.089；價量＋籌碼 0.128、0.107。

**訊號處理**（三種策略共用）：分數在流動性宇宙內取百分位 → 3 日半衰期指數平滑 → 每天對 60 日 beta、前 20 日與前 60 日報酬做橫斷面回歸取殘差。

| 策略 | 規則 | 參數（事前固定） | 曝險 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 多日多空 | 10 日分數；多頭 band（買進前 K、掉出前 m × K 才賣、每天最多進 K／10 檔）；空頭 band 用反向排序，受可借券名單、限額、借不到機率與分層借券費約束 | K = 50、m = 3（樣本內前段、上市、機構成本選定） | 金額中性，毛 2 倍 |
| 多空＋期貨避險 | 同上，加台指期 −β × 資本，β 為組合毛報酬對指數的前 60 日滾動 β | β 視窗 60 日 | 金額中性加 β 對沖 |
| 週頻低換手多頭 | 5 日分數；每個 ISO 週最後一個交易日再平衡；前 K 多頭 band、掉出前 m × K 才賣；每週最多換掉 50% × K，超過只賣分數最差的；停牌出場與期初建倉不計入上限 | K = 30、m = 2（兩個模型都選到這組） | 100% 多頭 |

**選定規則（事前）**：機構主要成本、兩市場各半、分數位置，樣本內淨 Sharpe 最高的（模型, 策略）格。**確認條件（事前）**：樣本外 (i) 年化淨報酬 > 0 且 (ii) 最大回撤不比樣本內深；兩者成立才進入模擬交易，否則維持研究狀態、不重選、不改建構。

### 8. 心得與改進方向

**流程上的心得**

1. **事前登記救了這個專案的可信度。** 七個階段裡大部分假說被否決；如果允許事後調整規則，幾乎每一步都能「做出」一個好結果。選定規則選出的策略在樣本外過不了，報告照實寫，才是這份工作有價值的地方。
2. **先查暴露，再怪模型。** 2025 年後的失效看起來像模型壞了，實際上是標籤與分數帶了市值、產業與 beta。兩次「取殘差」（標籤層、分數層）是全部改進裡最大的兩步，而且都沒有擬合參數。
3. **成本與執行限制必須在回測裡，不是在報告最後一段。** 每天換 10% 部位的策略在台股成本下不可能活；當沖被一個 tick 的價差否決；放空腿要過可借券名單、限額與借不到機率。這些都改變了結論。
4. **資料工程會以最安靜的方式毀掉結果。** 一次颱風休市沒有列進交易日曆，樣本規則就靜默丟掉了六個月的樣本；現在流程會在不一致時中止並列出日期。
5. **深度學習的增量要用 GPU 成本衡量。** 跨股票 transformer 比梯度提升樹多 0.004 的排序能力來自三個種子集成，不值一張 GPU 的時間；預訓練的時序基礎模型沒有轉移到台股截面。

### 1. Summary

This is a two-week (16 to 29 October 2026), fully pre-registered quantitative research project. It started from "fine-tune a financial time-series foundation model to predict Taiwan stock returns" and ended with a set of trading strategies backtested under real costs and execution limits and confirmed on a fully unseen out-of-sample window. In between came seven stages and twenty registered experiments; most hypotheses were rejected, and every rejection changed the next step.

The final outcome:

- **Model**: a LightGBM cross-sectional ranker on 183 price-volume features plus 67 free public chip features, predicting each stock's 5- to 10-day relative return net of market, size and industry effects; retrained every quarter on past data only. Chip data raises ranking skill significantly (5- to 10-day Rank IC +0.017, interval clear of zero).
- **Strategies**: three — multi-day long-short, long-short with a TAIEX futures hedge, weekly low-turnover long. In-sample (January 2023 to May 2026) they return +19% to +52% a year net of institution costs with net Sharpe 1.4 to 2.4.
- **The pre-registered selection rule** picks "price-volume + chip model × weekly low-turnover long" (in-sample Sharpe 2.38, +51.8% a year).
- **The out-of-sample confirmation (June to October 2026, 85 sessions) fails**: net return +3.5% is positive, but the −25.7% maximum drawdown is deeper than the in-sample −22.6%. A −17% market correction in July landed on a 100% long book; the market-neutral strategies made +23% to +34% in the same window, but the pre-registered rule does not allow a change of pick. **No strategy enters paper trading.**

The report records the whole path: what each stage did, why it failed, what was learned, and why the rule-selected strategy failed its confirmation. The most useful parts for a reader are probably the research history in section 4 and the lessons in section 8.

### 2. Problem and goals

**Question**: under strict point-in-time inputs, next-open execution and chronological evaluation, can a cross-sectional ranking model on daily price-volume plus free chip data become a strategy fit for paper trading under Taiwan's transaction costs and execution limits?

**Success criteria (pre-registered)**: institution primary cost, fractional capital, in-sample net Sharpe ≥ 1.0, net-return 95% lower bound > 0, second in-sample segment net return > 0; out-of-sample confirmation: net return > 0 and maximum drawdown not deeper than in-sample. Retail costs always listed alongside.

**Explicitly out of scope**: no paid data (user decision on 28 October); no LLM-driven factor mining; the day-trade route dropped once the cost evidence was in; "beating 0050 buy-and-hold" retired as a bar on 26 October — Taiwan since 2023 is a one-sided mega-cap rally, and passive holding and quantitative trading are different races.

### 4. Research history: seven stages

#### 4.1 Starting point: fine-tuning a financial time-series foundation model (before 16 October)

Kronos (a transformer pretrained on candlestick data) with LoRA, predicting US and Taiwan stocks' 1- to 14-day excess returns and direction. Result: no robust test-period edge across three seeds. An error decomposition showed the problem was not model capacity: alpha errors sat almost entirely in the within-day cross-section (the date level was 3% to 4%), and the direction classifier lost on unlearnable day-level market timing; with timing removed it matched gradient-boosted trees.

The first backtests (fixed holding periods, hold buffers) all failed: the validation-selected 10-day setting lost −37% to −44% a year net on test; the top names' edge existed only intraday (open to close +18 to +31 bps) and reversed overnight. A June to September 2026 forward test found no signal whose interval excluded zero.

**Lesson**: the model could separate relative strength within a day but not predict tomorrow's direction; the problem had to be restated as cross-sectional ranking.

#### 4.2 Restating the task as cross-sectional ranking (20 to 21 October)

Labels became within-day, within-market ranks. Five baselines (PatchTST, GRU, gradient-boosted trees, DLinear, moving-average crossover) mostly decayed from validation to test; the fine-tuned Kronos reached a Taiwan 1-day ranking skill of 0.064 (interval 0.027 to 0.102) **but did not beat PatchTST** (paired difference −0.002, interval spanning zero).

**Lesson**: the expensive foundation model is not the backbone for this problem; ranking skill comes from features and cross-sectional modelling. From here, GPU hours became the unit of cost in every decision.

#### 4.3 The Taiwan main line: feature gradient boosting against a cross-stock transformer (22 to 23 October)

Two models paired on one walk-forward: LightGBM on 183 hand-written features, and a MASTER-style cross-stock transformer (stocks of the same day attend to each other). LightGBM: short-horizon ranking skill 0.118, 5- to 10-day 0.086; the transformer 0.122 and 0.092, and the +0.004 gap came from the three-seed ensemble, not from inter-stock attention (an ablation proved it).

The first strategy (replace 5 of 50 names daily) failed under Taiwan costs: 10% daily turnover costs about 17% a year, more than the gross excess.

**Lesson**: deep learning's increment at this data size is not worth the GPU cost; the strategy layer's cost matters far more than +0.004 of ranking skill.

#### 4.4 Strategy layer, first round: hold buffers, futures hedge, day trading (23 October)

Replacing daily rotation with a buffer rule (buy into the top 50, sell only out of the top 150, at most 5 new names a day) cut turnover to 2% to 2.5% a day and the drag to 3% to 5% a year; the first in-sample segment's net excess was +5%, **but after June 2025 the gross excess was −27% to −31%**, and the whole window still failed. The TAIEX-futures-hedged version lost −12.5% a year with 28 margin calls (hedging a near-equal-weight book with a cap-weighted index is the wrong tool in a mega-cap rally).

A second route: the model's 1-day signal as a short-side day trade in single-stock futures. On official TAIFEX opens and settlements the gross return was +61.5% a year, but one tick of slippage per side (about 0.23%) made it −63% net — the spread ate everything.

**Lesson**: the cause of the post-2025 failure was still unknown; the day-trade route was rejected by execution cost, not by the model.

#### 4.5 Residualized labels (23 to 24 October)

Diagnosis: the post-June-2025 failure came from a concentrated large-cap rally. After regressing returns cross-sectionally on market, size and industry, the model's 5- to 10-day ranking skill on the **residual** stayed at 0.065 in the second segment (interval 0.046 to 0.086), while its skill on raw returns kept only 40% to 50% of the first segment's. Labels were switched to residualized returns and the model retrained: short-horizon skill unchanged, second-segment 5- to 10-day skill +0.009 (interval +0.006 to +0.013). Every later model uses this label. Cross-stock models with frozen pretrained encoders (Kronos-small, Chronos-2) retrained on it did not beat the from-scratch version, and the deep-learning route left the main line.

**Lesson**: the failure was the label carrying size and industry beta, not the model forgetting how to pick stocks.

#### 4.6 Exposure diagnostics and orthogonalization (24 to 26 October)

The single most important step. Correlating the smoothed 10-day score with 60-day beta and trailing 20- and 60-day returns gave −0.24 to −0.45 against beta and −0.1 to −0.26 against momentum: the score carried a hidden low-beta, anti-momentum tilt, and in the post-2025 momentum rally both legs stood on the wrong side (the short book was short the mega-cap winners and lost 61% to 89% a year).

**Regressing the score cross-sectionally each day on those three exposures and keeping the residual** (no fitted parameters, band parameters untouched) turned the same long-short strategy from −6.3% to **+17.3%** on TWSE (interval −0.6% to +37.3%), +19.4% on TPEX and +18.3% with both markets half each, net of institution costs; every post-2025 segment turned positive and book beta fell from −0.5 to −0.2; the futures hedge added +2% to +9%. Replacing the short book's "every name is borrowable" assumption with the real borrowable list, exchange quotas, borrow-failure probabilities and tiered fees left +15.3% (interval +2.5% to +30.3%); the list itself barely moved the result, the tiered fees and the failure draws each cost 1 to 3 points.

A "0050 core plus long-short overlay" was also tested: all 24 cells lost to 0050 buy-and-hold (+46% a year over the same window). The user retired that bar — passive mega-cap holding and quantitative trading are different races.

**Lesson**: when a strategy fails, check exposures before blaming the model. The orthogonalization was designed after seeing the second segment, so in-sample numbers can only be informational and a separate out-of-sample confirmation was required — which is where section 6 comes from.

#### 4.7 Free chip data (25 to 28 October)

67 chip features were built (multi-window cumulative ratios and z-scores of five institutional investor classes' net buying, margin and short-balance and lending changes, the day-trade ratio, attention and disposition status) under a conservative publication-time rule. Paired ablation: chip data raised 5- to 10-day residual ranking skill by +0.017 (interval +0.013 to +0.021) and the top-minus-bottom quartile 10-day spread from 0.65% to 1.04%, passing both pre-registered gates; a chip-only model's post-2025 5- to 10-day skill (0.090) even exceeded the 183 price-volume columns' (0.065). The gain is larger among recent winners but significant in both periods and groups, so it is an independent signal rather than only a conditional reversal.

User decision: no purchase of paid broker-branch data for now; build the strategies and the out-of-sample confirmation with the models in hand.

### 5. Final model and strategy design

**Model**: LightGBM ranker in two paired versions — price-volume (183 columns) and price-volume + chip (250 columns); residualized labels; three-seed ensemble; 16-fold quarterly walk-forward expanding from 2016. Ranking skill (791 in-sample days): price-volume short 0.117, 5- to 10-day 0.089; price-volume + chip 0.128, 0.107.

**Signal processing** (shared by the three strategies): within-universe percentile → 3-day half-life exponential smoothing → daily cross-sectional regression on 60-day beta and trailing 20- and 60-day returns, keeping the residual.

| Strategy | Rule | Parameters (fixed in advance) | Exposure |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Multi-day long-short | 10-day score; long band (buy into the top K, sell out of the top m × K, at most K/10 entries a day); short band on the reversed ranking under the borrowable list, quota, borrow-failure probability and tiered fees | K = 50, m = 3 (selected on the first in-sample segment, TWSE, institution cost) | dollar neutral, gross 2× |
| Long-short + futures hedge | as above plus TAIEX futures at −β × capital, β the book's trailing 60-day rolling beta to the index | β window 60 days | dollar neutral plus β hedge |
| Weekly low-turnover long | 5-day score; rebalanced on the last session of each ISO week; top-K long band, sell only out of the top m × K; at most 50% × K replacements a week, worst-ranked first; forced exits on halts and the initial build outside the cap | K = 30, m = 2 (both models selected this pair) | 100% long |

**Selection rule (pre-registered)**: institution primary cost, both markets half each, fractional capital; the (model, strategy) cell with the highest in-sample net Sharpe. **Confirmation (pre-registered)**: out of sample, (i) annualized net return > 0 and (ii) maximum drawdown not deeper than in-sample; both for paper trading, otherwise research status with no re-selection and no construction change.

### 7. Data limitations (stated in advance)

(a) Adjusted prices carry dividends only, not splits, capital reductions or par-value changes: 109 stocks since 2023 show a single-day adjusted-open jump above 25% (48 events across 42 stocks inside the out-of-sample window). Position returns use adjacent sessions' opens and exit on halts, so they are unaffected; the model's training labels and price features around the events are wrong. The correction is implemented but not yet retrained.
(b) The second in-sample segment had been seen when the orthogonalization was designed, so in-sample numbers are informational; the only real test is the 85 out-of-sample days. The weekly long is new, its parameters were selected on the first segment, and both the second segment and the out-of-sample window are unseen for it.
(c) The out-of-sample window has 85 sessions in one market regime, with intervals ±60 to ±110 points wide.
(d) Chip-feature publication times follow a conservative rule (before the next open); the foreign-holding z-score column is always missing because of a source issue.
(e) The short book's borrow-failure, recall and fee tiers are parameter assumptions.
(f) The out-of-sample window contains the April 2025 crash and the July 2026 correction but not the 2022 bear market; broker-branch and shareholder-dispersion data were not bought.

### 8. Lessons and improvement directions

**Process lessons**

1. **Pre-registration saved the project's credibility.** Most hypotheses across the seven stages were rejected; with rules adjustable after the fact almost every step could have "produced" a good result. The rule-selected strategy failing out of sample, reported as it is, is what makes the work worth anything.
2. **Check exposures before blaming the model.** The post-2025 failure looked like a broken model and was in fact size, industry and beta riding inside the labels and scores. The two residualizations (label level, score level) were the two largest improvements of the whole project, and neither has a fitted parameter.
3. **Costs and execution limits belong inside the backtest, not in a closing paragraph.** A strategy replacing 10% of its book daily cannot live under Taiwan costs; day trading was killed by one tick of spread; the short book had to pass the borrowable list, quotas and failure draws. Each changed the conclusion.
4. **Data engineering ruins results in the quietest way.** One typhoon closure missing from the trading calendar made the sample rule silently drop six months of samples; the pipeline now stops and lists the dates on any mismatch.
5. **Measure deep learning's increment in GPU hours.** The cross-stock transformer's +0.004 over gradient boosting came from a three-seed ensemble and was not worth a GPU's time; pretrained time-series foundation models did not transfer to the Taiwan cross-section.

**Improvement directions (in order, each registered before it is run)**

| Order | Item | Reason |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | The weekly long with a beta hedge or a volatility target, or the market-neutral L/S + hedge as the main line with a re-registered confirmation period | The selected strategy failed on market beta; the market-neutral versions made +23% to +34% in the same window |
| 2 | Retrain on split- and capital-reduction-adjusted data and recompute everything | Section 7 (a) |
| 3 | Event study: reversals after disposition listings, first attention listings and day-trade-ratio jumps | Unused event-type signals in the free data |
| 4 | Retrain the cross-stock transformer on the chip features (user decision) | The chip increment holds at the ranking level; the DL increment is concentrated in 2023 to 2024 |
| 5 | Buy broker-branch data (user decision; both pre-registered gates passed) | Literature and this project's sub-analysis point to branch data separating continuation from reversal among recent winners |
