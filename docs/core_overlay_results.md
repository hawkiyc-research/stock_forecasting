# §13.17 補充：0050 核心加正交化多空疊加結果 / 0050 Core plus Orthogonalized Long-Short Overlay: Results

## 中文

### 1. 目的與結論

[實驗協定 §13.17 補充](experiment_protocol.md)把 S1 重新登記為「0050 買進持有核心，加上 §13.19 補充二的正交化現股多空疊加」，門檻 H-G 是勝過 0050 買進持有：選定組合在主要成本與分數位置下，P1 ∪ P2「策略減 0050」年化淨差的 95% 區間下界 > 0，且 P2 單獨淨差 > 0；機構與散戶分別判。多空兩腿沿用 §13.19 補充四的主要假設（可借券名單與次日限額、借不到機率 × 1、分層借券費、五個隨機重複平均），band 參數固定、不重選。變體 A 以 0050 現股為核心、多頭腿不足的現金以 0050 質押借款支應；變體 B 以台指期多頭提供 β、現金保證金 15%。本報告是資訊性判讀：V1 設計在看過 P2 之後決定，正式判定在 P3。

結論：

1. **兩個變體、兩個模型、三個市場、兩個成本檔共 24 格，H-G 全部未通過。** 沒有任何一格的「策略減 0050」區間下界 > 0。最接近的是 D0 TPEX 機構的變體 B：+56.6% 對 0050 +46.2%、差 +10.4% [−3.4%, +25.7%]、P2 差 −0.6%；B0 TPEX 機構的變體 B 差 +6.1% [−6.5%, +18.7%]、P2 差 +15.3%。依 §13.17 補充第 7 項的事前規則，結論維持「沒有勝過 0050 買進持有的可部署策略」。
2. **0050 本身太強。** 2023-01 到 2026-05 的 0050 買進持有（股息再投入、含分割調整、機構成本）年化 +46.2%（CAGR +55%）、Sharpe 2.15、最大回撤 −25.2%，P2 年化 +95.7%；同期 TAIEX 報酬指數年化 +36.9%，0050 因台積電權重再贏指數 9 個百分點。變體 A 把 30% 的資本從 0050 移到多空疊加，少賺 0.3 × 46% ≈ 14 個百分點，而多空腿（每腿 1 倍）在同一成本下只賺 +15% 再扣 1.7% 融資利息，兩者相抵；變體 B 的台指期多頭淨年化 +37.9%，輸 0050 8 個百分點，0.85 倍的多空腿只夠補回這個差距再多 3 到 6 個百分點，區間跨 0。
3. **選到的變體 A 在 60% 質押成數下不可融資。** 兩個模型、三個市場、兩個成本檔都在 P1 選到 w = 0.7 與最大的 L（機構 1.0、散戶 0.5）：機構組合的借款 0.7 對上 0.7 的 0050 持股，起始質押成數 100%，前 364 個交易日（整個 P1 前段）擔保品不足，要等 0050 漲到 1.67 倍才合規；在 60% 成數下 w = 0.7 可行的 L 上限約 0.72，而可行的 L = 0.5 格在 TW 輸 0050 6.7 個百分點（區間 [−16.3%, +3.5%]）。散戶 L = 0.5 的變體 A 輸 8 到 14 個百分點，D0 TW 與 TWSE 的區間上界 < 0。
4. **疊加贏的是風險，不是報酬。** 變體 A 的 Sharpe 2.4 到 2.8（0050 2.15）、最大回撤 −13% 到 −20%（0050 −25%）；它把 0050 的 2024-08 回撤砍掉一半，代價是 P2 大漲時少賺 2 到 37 個百分點。對以絕對報酬對 0050 計分的委託，這不是贏；對以回撤計分的，它是改善，但不是本節登記的門檻。
5. **資本情境。** 機構 3,000 萬到 3 億的變體 A（TW、選定參數）對 0050 的差 −2 到 −8 個百分點，名目部署率 81% 到 98%；散戶 1,000 萬（含零股）−8 到 −12 個百分點。沒有因資金規模改變結論。
6. **資料發現。** 資料集的 `adjusted_close` 只含股息、不含分割與減資：0050 於 2025-06-18 一拆四，第一次計算把 P2 的 0050 算成 −49% 的 CAGR；修正後（`core.splits`）才是上面的數字。全檔 2023 年起有 109 檔個股出現超過 25% 的單日還原開盤跳動（多為停牌 12 日後的減資或分割），但 band 引擎只在同一檔相鄰訊號日之間計算開盤到開盤報酬、停牌即出場，§13.14 到 §13.19 的部位報酬最大單日 |移動| 26%，沒有任何一筆跳動進入部位；標籤與特徵仍可能含這些跳動，列為資料管線的待辦。

### 4. 判讀

1. **H-G 全部未通過，依事前規則不建策略。** 24 格沒有一格的區間下界 > 0；變體 A 的 P2 差全部為負，變體 B 只有 B0 的 P2 差為正（+6% 到 +15%）但 P1 ∪ P2 的下界 −6.5% 到 −12.8%。
2. **問題在基準，不在疊加。** 多空腿本身在同一執行層下 P1 ∪ P2 淨 +15%（§13.19 補充四），Sharpe 1.2 到 1.7，這是一條獨立於市場的報酬；但 0050 同期年化 +46%，任何從 0050 移出的資本都要用 46% 的機會成本去換 15% 的 alpha。變體 B 以台指期保留 1 倍 β，alpha 腿才真的疊上去（+4% 到 +10%），但台指期輸 0050 8 個百分點（台積電集中度），把增量吃掉一半，剩下的在區間內。
3. **選擇規則選到不可融資的組合。** P1 以 IR 選 (w, L) 時，最高 L 一律勝出，因為多空腿在 P1 的 Sharpe 高；但 w = 0.7、L = 1.0 的借款等於 0050 持股全額，60% 成數下 364 天擔保不足。這是事前設計沒有把融資可行性寫進選擇規則的缺陷；報告照實列出，不另改規則重選。可行的格（L = 0.5）在 TW 機構輸 0050 6.7 個百分點。
4. **風險面的改善是真的。** 變體 A 把最大回撤由 −25% 壓到 −13% 到 −20%、Sharpe 由 2.15 提到 2.4 到 2.8；變體 B 回撤與 0050 相當（台指期與 0050 同向）。如果委託的目標是回撤或 Sharpe 而不是對 0050 的絕對差，變體 A 是合理的組合；但那不是本節登記的門檻，也需要 P3 確認。
5. **與事前預期（第 7 項）的對照。** 事前粗估變體 A ≈ +37% 對 0050 +41%、變體 B ≈ +53%；實際 0050 含股息與分割調整後 +46.2%，變體 A 選定格 +45.9%、變體 B +50.9%（B0 TW 機構）。幅度與方向都如預期：贏不贏取決於多空腿在可借券名單內剩多少與基準的強度，結果是打平與小贏、區間跨 0。

### 5. 判讀限制

- P2 是 0050 最強的一段（年化 +95.7%）而且已經看過；用它當基準是最難的考法，也是看過答案後的設計。
- 質押借款利率 2.5%／4.0%、60% 成數、借券費分層與借不到機率都是假設值；散戶變體以融券執行，融券額度與停券事件只以名單與限額模擬。
- 台指期多頭以結算價記帳、現股以開盤到開盤，含時點噪音；15% 保證金低於 10% 補足的規則沒有在本樣本觸發。
- 多空腿五個重複的空頭腿年化極差 4 到 8 個百分點，表中的「策略減 0050」差帶 ±1 到 2 個百分點的抽樣雜訊。
- 資料集的還原價不含分割與減資，0050 已修正；個股的標籤與特徵可能受 109 檔跳動影響，band 部位報酬未受影響。

### 6. 處置

依 §13.17 補充第 7 項：兩個變體都不能在 P1 ∪ P2 贏過 0050，結論維持「沒有勝過 0050 買進持有的可部署策略」。多空疊加本身（§13.19 補充二、補充四）仍是達到資訊性門檻的市場中性組合，P3 的判定對象是那個組合，不是本節的 0050 疊加；本節在 P3 以同一設定重算一次作對照，不再調整 (w, L) 或選擇規則。資料管線的待辦：`adjusted_close` 加入分割與減資調整。

### 1. Purpose and conclusions

[Experiment protocol §13.17 supplement](experiment_protocol.md) re-registered S1 as "a 0050 buy-and-hold core plus the orthogonalized stock long-short overlay of §13.19 supplement 2". The bar H-G is beating 0050 buy and hold: for the selected combination, under primary costs and fractional capital, the 95% interval of the P1 ∪ P2 annual net difference "strategy minus 0050" must clear 0 and the P2 difference alone must be positive, judged separately for the institution and retail cost profiles. The two legs use §13.19 supplement 4's primary assumptions (borrowable lists and next-session quota, failure probabilities × 1, tiered borrow fees, five random replicates averaged); the band parameters are fixed, not re-selected. Variant A holds 0050 itself and funds the long leg's shortfall with a loan secured on the 0050 holding; variant B takes β from a long TX position on a 15% cash margin. The reading is informational: the V1 design was chosen after seeing P2, the formal decision is P3.

Conclusions:

1. **H-G fails in all 24 cells (two variants × two models × three markets × two cost profiles).** No cell has a lower bound of "strategy minus 0050" above 0. The closest is D0 TPEX institution variant B: +56.6% against 0050's +46.2%, difference +10.4% [−3.4%, +25.7%], P2 difference −0.6%; B0 TPEX institution variant B differs by +6.1% [−6.5%, +18.7%] with a P2 difference of +15.3%. Under the pre-registered rule of §13.17 supplement item 7 the conclusion stays "no deployable strategy beats 0050 buy and hold".
2. **0050 itself is too strong.** From 2023-01 to 2026-05, 0050 buy and hold (dividends reinvested, split-adjusted, institution costs) earns +46.2% a year (CAGR +55%), Sharpe 2.15, maximum drawdown −25.2%, and +95.7% a year in P2; the TAIEX total-return index earns +36.9% a year over the same span, so 0050 beats the index by another 9 points through its TSMC weight. Variant A moves 30% of the capital from 0050 into the overlay and gives up 0.3 × 46% ≈ 14 points, while the long-short legs (at 1× each) earn +15% under the same costs minus 1.7% of loan interest: the two cancel. Variant B's long TX earns +37.9% net, 8 points behind 0050, and 0.85× of the long-short only covers that gap plus 3 to 6 points, inside the interval.
3. **The selected variant A cannot be financed at a 60% loan-to-value.** Every (model, market, profile) selects w = 0.7 with the largest L (institution 1.0, retail 0.5) on P1: the institution combination borrows 0.7 against a 0.7 holding of 0050, a 100% loan-to-value at the start, short of collateral for the first 364 sessions (most of P1) until 0050 has risen 1.67×; at a 60% limit the feasible L at w = 0.7 is about 0.72, and the feasible L = 0.5 cell trails 0050 by 6.7 points on TW ([−16.3%, +3.5%]). The retail variant A (L = 0.5) trails by 8 to 14 points, with upper bounds below 0 for D0 on TW and TWSE.
4. **The overlay wins on risk, not on return.** Variant A's Sharpe is 2.4 to 2.8 (0050: 2.15) and its maximum drawdown −13% to −20% (0050: −25%); it halves 0050's August-2024 drawdown at the price of earning 2 to 37 points less in P2's surge. For a mandate scored on absolute return against 0050 that is not a win; for one scored on drawdown it is an improvement, but not the bar registered here.
5. **Capital scenarios.** Institution NT$30M to NT$300M variant A (TW, selected parameters) trails 0050 by 2 to 8 points at 81% to 98% deployed; retail NT$10M (odd lots allowed) by 8 to 12 points. Size does not change the conclusion.
6. **A data finding.** The dataset's `adjusted_close` carries dividends only, not splits or capital reductions: 0050 split 1:4 on 2025-06-18 and the first run scored its P2 as a −49% CAGR; the numbers above follow the correction (`core.splits`). ### 4. Reading

1. **H-G fails everywhere, so no strategy is built, as pre-registered.** None of the 24 cells has a lower bound above 0; every variant A P2 difference is negative, and variant B's P2 difference is positive only for B0 (+6% to +15%) while its P1 ∪ P2 lower bounds are −6.5% to −12.8%.
2. **The problem is the benchmark, not the overlay.** Under the same execution layer the long-short legs earn +15% net on P1 ∪ P2 (§13.19 supplement 4) with a Sharpe of 1.2 to 1.7, a return independent of the market; but 0050 earned +46% a year over the same span, so every unit of capital moved out of 0050 trades a 46% opportunity cost for 15% of alpha. Variant B keeps 1× β through TX, so the alpha leg really is added (+4% to +10%), but TX trails 0050 by 8 points (TSMC concentration), which eats half of the gain and leaves the rest inside the interval.
3. **The selection rule picked a combination that cannot be financed.** Choosing (w, L) on P1 by IR always favours the largest L, because the long-short's P1 Sharpe is high; but w = 0.7 with L = 1.0 borrows the full value of the 0050 holding, short of collateral for 364 sessions at a 60% loan-to-value. This is a flaw of the pre-registered design, which did not write financing feasibility into the selection rule; the report lists it as is and does not re-select under a changed rule. The feasible cell (L = 0.5) trails 0050 by 6.7 points on TW for the institution.
4. **The risk improvement is real.** Variant A cuts the maximum drawdown from −25% to −13% to −20% and lifts the Sharpe from 2.15 to 2.4 to 2.8; variant B's drawdown matches 0050's (TX and 0050 move together). For a mandate whose target is drawdown or Sharpe rather than the absolute difference to 0050, variant A is a reasonable portfolio; but that is not the bar registered here, and it would need P3.
5. **Against the pre-registered expectation (item 7).** The rough estimate was variant A ≈ +37% against 0050 +41% and variant B ≈ +53%; with dividends and the split correction 0050 earned +46.2%, the selected variant A +45.9% and variant B +50.9% (B0 TW institution). Direction and size are as expected: whether the overlay wins depends on how much of the long-short survives the borrowable list and on the benchmark's strength, and the outcome is a tie or a small win with intervals spanning 0.

### 5. Reading limits

- P2 is 0050's strongest stretch (+95.7% a year) and has been seen; it is the hardest benchmark and a design chosen after the answer.
- The loan rates 2.5% / 4.0%, the 60% loan-to-value, the fee tiers and the failure probabilities are assumptions; the retail variant executes shorts through margin, whose quotas and suspensions are simulated through the lists and quota only.
- Long TX is marked at settlements while stocks run open to open, which adds timing noise; the 15% margin's refill rule below 10% did not trigger in this sample.
- The short leg's annual return spans 4 to 8 points across the five replicates, so the "strategy minus 0050" differences carry ±1 to 2 points of sampling noise.
- The dataset's adjusted prices ignore splits and capital reductions; 0050 is corrected, stock labels and features may still be affected by the 109 jumps, the band position returns are not.

### 6. Disposition

Under §13.17 supplement item 7: neither variant beats 0050 on P1 ∪ P2, so the conclusion stays "no deployable strategy beats 0050 buy and hold". The long-short overlay itself (§13.19 supplements 2 and 4) remains the market-neutral portfolio that meets the informational bar; P3 decides on that portfolio, not on this 0050 combination, which is rerun once on P3 with the same settings for comparison, without re-tuning (w, L) or the selection rule. Data-pipeline follow-up: add split and capital-reduction adjustments to `adjusted_close`.
